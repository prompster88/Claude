## Category Marketing Rules

### Footwear — Always Do
- Hero visual: single shoe, sharp detail shot OR styled on feet
- Mention brand of footwear if curated (Chie Mihara, Officine Creative, Marsell etc.)
- Price point is a feature, not a barrier — these are investment pieces
- Retargeting carousel = highest footwear ROAS

### Pants — Always Do
- Link to size guide in every ad and post
- Emphasize fit language: "מותאם לגוף של נשים אמיתיות"
- Show multiple sizes if possible
- Avoid creating expectation that can't be met → return rate spike

### Sale / Discounts — Tone Guide
- Never: "מבצע מטורף!" or urgency panic
- Yes: "בחרנו. הוזלנו. עכשיו אצלך."
- The brand chose to discount — it's a gift, not a clearance
- Headline currently used on site: "תוכניות לחוד ומציאות לחוד" — reference this framing

## Loyalty Program

**מועדון חברות** — active membership loyalty program
- Mention in content as a benefit, not a gimmick
- "חברות מקבלות — [benefit]" framing preferred over "הצטרפי עכשיו!!!"
- Entry point for email capture and retention marketing
