# Comme Il Faut — Stores, Locations & Physical Footprint

> Last verified: August 2026, via public web sources (official site pages, Israeli press, business directories).
> ⚠️ The brand's physical footprint changed dramatically in 2025. Anything written before 2025 about
> "the Tel Aviv Port flagship" is **obsolete**. Never write copy that sends customers to the Port.

---

## 1. The 2025 relocation — the single most important fact

| | |
|---|---|
| **What happened** | After **~20 years**, Comme Il Faut closed its iconic Tel Aviv Port complex (Beit Banamal / בית בנמל, Hangar 26). The lease was not renewed. |
| **When** | Farewell clearance sale ran **5–25 January 2025** (up to 80% off — furniture, vintage, books, samples, archive pieces). Store closed shortly after. |
| **Why (stated)** | Rising rents at the Port + fast-fashion chains taking over the compound; the Port lost its identity as a home for small independent businesses. |
| **What was lost** | The Port site was a cultural institution, not just a store: fashion house + **women-only spa** + **chef restaurant** + gallery + the COMME IL FAUT VINTAGE shop. |
| **Where they went** | New flagship at **Levontin 28, Tel Aviv** (Gan HaChashmal neighbourhood), opened ~spring 2025. |

**Narrative framing for copy:** the move is deliberately "from north Tel Aviv to the south of the city" —
from an affluent, aging, mall-adjacent audience to a young, urban, authentic neighbourhood full of small
boutiques and wine bars. Press framed it as *"היי שלום צפון ת״א, עכשיו זמן דרום העיר."*

## 2. Current locations

### 🏛️ Flagship — Levontin 28, Tel Aviv (Gan HaChashmal)
- **Address:** לבונטין 28, תל אביב (רחוב לבונטין / אזור גן החשמל)
- **Opened:** ~2025, replacing the Tel Aviv Port flagship
- **Building:** a **preservation / heritage building** (בניין לשימור)
- **Contains:** the store + an **art gallery** + an **inner courtyard (חצר)** used for events
- **Programming intent:** performances by emerging artists, talks, community meet-ups and content aimed
  at a younger female audience — the courtyard is explicitly a cultural venue, not just retail space
- **Strategic role:** brand HQ of experience; where new collections (incl. Daniel Cohen's SS26) debut
- *Confidence note:* opening hours/phone for this branch were not confirmable from a public source
  during this refresh — verify on the site or in-store before publishing them.

### 🏭 The Studio — Kibbutz Galuyot 29, Tel Aviv
- **Address:** קיבוץ גלויות 29, תל אביב
- **Public name:** COMME IL FAUT STUDIO — אאוטלט + וינטג׳
- **What's inside:** offices, the **design room**, the warehouse, the **photo studio**,
  the **archive of garments dating back to 1987**, an outlet/overstock shop,
  and a **vintage department** with special collector pieces
- **Why it matters for content:** this is the brand's most story-rich location — archive, atelier and
  resale under one roof. It is the natural set for behind-the-scenes, archive-revival and slow-fashion content.

### 🌊 Haifa — pop-up inside Anicha, Carmel Center
- **Address:** מוריה 3, מרכז הכרמל, חיפה — inside the **Anicha (אניצ׳ה)** pop-up space
- **Location cues:** opposite Gan Mania, next to the Cinematheque and Auditorium; the "Birdy" café is across the road
- **Hours (as published):** א׳–ה׳ 10:00–19:00 · ו׳ 9:30–14:00
- **Parking:** blue-and-white street parking; small lot on Kler St. near Supersol; Auditorium parking.
  Transit: Carmelit to Gan Ha'em, then a short walk.
- **Status:** a **pop-up / host-space partnership**, not a permanent branch. It has run in seasonal
  waves (an initial run through end of December, then a return). The site announced it as
  "New store – Haifa!", but treat it as **pop-up until re-verified** — always confirm it is currently
  open before driving traffic to it.
- **Signature detail:** the pop-up carried a prominent **"FUCK FAST FASHION"** sign as its statement piece.

### 🛍️ MAKERS OUTLET — Herzliya (a Comme Il Faut venture, multi-designer)
- **Address:** מדינת היהודים 85, הרצליה פיתוח (Azrieli Outlet Herzliya)
- **Hours (as published):** א׳–ה׳ 10:00–21:00 · ו׳ 9:00–15:00 · שבת 10:00–22:00
- **What it is:** a large designed outlet **"from the house of Comme Il Faut"** selling overstock from
  leading **Israeli** designers — described in press as "the younger, wilder sister of Beit Banamal"
- **Brands carried (partial):** Gertrude, Alma Banker, Eva Mendelbaum, Rakefet Levi, Bianco Nero, Gusta,
  Maria Berman, Alef Alef, Bickel, Comme Il Faut, Sabon (soaps)
- **Positioning note:** MAKERS is a **separate brand/venture**. Do not blur its voice with CIF's own line,
  and do not imply CIF's main collection is sold at outlet prices.

## 4. Practical rules for using locations in content

- ✅ Default store CTA is **Levontin 28** — it is the flagship, the gallery, the courtyard, the story.
- ✅ When writing "visit us", name the specific branch. Never write a generic "our stores" list
  without checking which ones are actually open that season.
- ✅ The Studio (Kibbutz Galuyot 29) is for **archive / vintage / outlet / BTS** stories, not for
  full-price collection launches.
- ✅ Haifa and any pop-up need a **date-bounded** message ("until X", "this month only") — pop-ups end.
- ❌ Never mention the Tel Aviv Port as a current location. It is nostalgia, and only ever nostalgia.
- ❌ Never use MAKERS Outlet's multi-brand discount voice on the CIF main line.
- 🗺️ Geo-targeting for paid media: Tel Aviv core (Levontin/Gan HaChashmal, Rabin Sq.),
  Ramat HaSharon/north Sharon, Rehovot/Shfela, Herzliya (MAKERS), Haifa only while a pop-up is live.
