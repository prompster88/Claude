## 0. Quick checklist for a women's-health / wellness campaign

1. **No curative, preventive or treatment claims** for food or supplements ("relieves hot flashes", "prevents osteoporosis"). No consumer advertising of prescription drugs, including HRT.
2. **TV and radio health ads need a medical opinion and possibly pre-clearance.** Add clearance time to the schedule (**(UNVERIFIED)** roughly 1–3 weeks). Brand-level and lifestyle messaging clears more easily.
3. **No doctors endorsing products or services.** Also no patient testimonials with names or faces, no celebrities endorsing medical services, and no actors in white coats without a prominent disclaimer.
4. **Influencers:** put a prominent "פרסומת" / "שיתוף פעולה בתשלום" label at the start of the content or use the platform's paid-partnership tool. The advertiser is liable for what its agency does.
5. **Email/SMS/WhatsApp:** get documented opt-in, start the message or subject with "פרסומת", identify the sender and give an easy way to unsubscribe. The risk is NIS 1,000 per message.
6. **Data:** quiz answers and symptom data count as *health data* under Privacy Amendment 13. Minimize what goes to pixels and CAPI, and don't build retargeting pools from symptom pages.
7. **Platforms:** Meta may block lower-funnel optimization for health domains. Google blocks remarketing and Customer Match for health-condition ads.
8. **Calendar and risk:** watch election and war contingencies, and get pre-emption, pause and cancellation clauses into every deal.

## 1. Second Authority for TV & Radio

- **Main TV instrument:** כללי הרשות השנייה (תוכן פרסומי בטלוויזיה), תשפ"ב-2021. The older rules are the ethics rules (1994), scheduling rules (1992) and sponsorship rules (2009). Radio has its own ethics and scheduling rules (1999). **(UNVERIFIED)** whether the older rules were fully replaced.
  - https://www.nevo.co.il/law_html/law00/204875.htm · https://www.nevo.co.il/law_html/law00/4944.htm · https://www.nevo.co.il/law_html/law00/74022.htm
- **Ad-minute caps:** sources conflict.
  - The older scheduling rules cap advertising at about 10% of daily broadcast time.
  - In 20:00–24:00, broadcasters may spread up to 40 minutes of ads at their discretion.
  - The press cites up to 12 minutes an hour.
  - Ask the sales houses for current break structures. Prime-time news breaks are cluttered, which is why first/last-in-break carries a premium.
  - https://www.nevo.co.il/law_html/law00/4941.htm
- **Sponsorship:** allowed, with limits on length, form and product appearance. **(UNVERIFIED)** the exact seconds.
  - https://www.nevo.co.il/law_html/law00/73408.htm
- **Health, medicine and treatment ads:**
  - The advertiser must supply a suitable medical opinion supporting the ad's reliability.
  - An independent expert or authorized body may need to approve the ad in advance.
  - Ads for prescription-only medicines and treatments are prohibited.
  - Some therapies need the director's approval.
  - Supplement and vitamin ads aimed at children are restricted.

## 2. Kan (public broadcaster)

- **Rules:** כללי מועצת תאגיד השידור הישראלי (שידור פרסומות, הודעות חסות ותשדירים לשירות הציבור), תשפ"א-2021 — https://www.nevo.co.il/law_html/law00/201776.htm
- **Kan radio** (Bet, Gimel, 88 and others) carries commercials, up to 9 minutes an hour. Radio sponsorship mentions run up to 10 seconds.
- **Kan 11 TV** carries **sponsorship announcements only**, up to 6 seconds. A written sponsor mention may fill at most one-third of the screen for at most 10 seconds.
- **Kan digital** carries no ads.
- **Caps:** one advertiser may take at most 10% of daily TV ad time or 15% on radio. A media buyer may take at most 30% on radio.
- **Planner's view:** Kan Gimel and 88 reach a 35–60 female audience with little clutter. Kan's future is politically uncertain.

## 3. Ministry of Health

**Food and supplements.**
- **Governing rules:** תקנות בריאות הציבור (מזון)(תוספי תזונה) 1997, the Protection of Public Health (Food) Law 2015, and the older ban on attributing healing properties to food.
- **No approved health-claims list exists,** so there is an effectively blanket ban on curative or preventive claims. Class actions over claims are common.
- **The Ministry is watching social-media supplement ads.** In November 2025 it warned about fake-endorsement weight-loss capsules.
- https://www.gov.il/he/departments/legalinfo/health-mazon31a · https://www.israelhayom.co.il/health/article/19327774

**Medicines and HRT.**
- Prescription-drug advertising to the public is prohibited. HRT is prescription-only, so it cannot be advertised by brand.
- Over-the-counter drug ads must follow the Pharmacists (Preparations) Regulations 1986.
- Unbranded disease-awareness content that is linked to pharma is a grey area. Get legal advice. **(UNVERIFIED)**
- https://www.nevo.co.il/law_html/law00/4850.htm · https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s13584-017-0175-y

**Medical devices.** Under the Medical Equipment Law 2012, devices must be registered with AMAR before they are advertised. This covers pelvic-floor gadgets and wearables that make medical claims. https://www.nevo.co.il/law_html/law01/500_700.htm

**Doctors in ads.**
- The Doctors Regulations (Prohibited Advertising) 2008 prohibit, among other things:
  - success statistics and promised results;
  - praise of a doctor's skill;
  - celebrities;
  - named patient testimonials;
  - people who look like doctors or patients, unless the ad discloses they are not;
  - before/after images of intimate areas.
- The Israel Medical Association's ethics code bars doctors from promoting commercial products.
- **Implication:** Hili's physicians (e.g. the CMO) can appear in *educational content*. Avoid them endorsing paid packages in ads, and get legal review of any gray-zone format.
- https://www.nevo.co.il/law_html/law00/73265.htm · https://www.the7eye.org.il/370896

## 4. Consumer Protection Law 1981

- **§7 prohibits misleading advertising.** The Authority can impose administrative fines, and class-action risk is high.
- **Influencers:** any incentive (money, free product, a trip) makes the content advertising, and it must be disclosed prominently at the start. A hashtag buried at the end is not enough. Fake reviews and selectively hiding reviews are prohibited. The draft guidance dates from January 2021. **(UNVERIFIED)** whether it was finalized.
  - https://herzoglaw.co.il/en/news-and-insights/cpa_draft/
- **Landmark case (Feb 2023):**
  - Abbott (Similac) was fined NIS 5.53M, and its agency De Liders about NIS 0.5M.
  - The case involved fake profiles seeding about 100 Facebook mothers' groups.
  - **Lesson:** "community seeding" in women's groups is high-risk, and the advertiser is liable for its agency.
  - https://www.calcalist.co.il/shopping/article/bkn0eecaj

## 5. Anti-spam — Communications Law §30A

- **Scope:** email, SMS, WhatsApp and other instant messaging, auto-dialing and fax.
- **Consent:** prior explicit consent is required.
- **Required format:** "פרסומת" at the start of the message or subject, the sender's identity and contact details, and a free way to unsubscribe.
- **Limited exception:** existing customers who were offered an opt-out and are sent similar products.
- **Damages:** up to NIS 1,000 per message with no need to prove harm.
- **Practical:** WhatsApp broadcast lists count. Keep consent logs.
- https://www.gov.il/en/departments/faq/17052018_7

## 6. Platform policies (global)

- **Meta:**
  - Health-condition detailed targeting has been gone since 2022.
  - Since January 2025, health and wellness domains can be "partially restricted", which blocks optimization for Purchase, Lead and similar lower-funnel events.
  - Custom events and audiences that reference health attributes may be disabled.
  - **Plan:** optimize for Landing Page View or engagement, use on-platform lead forms and Advantage+ broad audiences, and consider keeping sales pages separate from symptom-content pages.
  - Ads implying knowledge of the user's condition ("Suffering from menopause?") violate the personal-attributes policy.
  - https://transparency.meta.com/policies/ad-standards/restricted-goods-services/health-wellness/
- **Google:**
  - Health is a sensitive category, so health-condition ads cannot use remarketing, Customer Match or custom segments.
  - Contextual, keyword and predefined audiences still work.
  - https://support.google.com/adspolicy/answer/143465?hl=en

## 7. Privacy Protection Law — Amendment 13 (in force 14 Aug 2025)

- **Broader definitions:** online identifiers and profiling data now count as personal data. A new category of "information of special sensitivity" covers health, genetic, biometric and location data.
- **Enforcement powers:** administrative fines, reported at up to about NIS 320K per violation with scaling (**(UNVERIFIED)** the exact caps), plus a right to sue without proving damage.
- **Criminal exposure:** collecting data by deceit, such as fake "gift" forms used for mailing.
- **Fines so far:** HOT NIS 70K, and a health fund NIS 256K (July 2026).
- **Cookies:** the Authority recommends opt-in. With health data involved, opt-in is the prudent choice.
- https://www.linklaters.com/en/insights/data-protected/data-protected---israel · https://www.pearlcohen.com/the-israeli-privacy-protection-authority-recommends-obtaining-user-consent-to-cookies/

## 8. Market-context events (2026) — re-check at time of use

- **Broadcasting overhaul law (passed 16 July 2026):**
  - It will replace the Second Authority and the Cable & Satellite Council with a single regulator.
  - The High Court froze key parts, including commercials on HOT/yes.
  - Most of it takes effect about two years out, so **current Second Authority rules still apply** for 2026 plans.
  - If ads on HOT/yes are ever allowed, TV inventory grows and prices could soften.
  - https://www.timesofisrael.com/high-court-freezes-key-parts-of-governments-controversial-media-overhaul-law/
- **Knesset elections on 27 Oct 2026:**
  - TV and radio election broadcasts are allotted slots from about **13 Oct**. **(UNVERIFIED)**
  - The direct squeeze on commercial spots is limited. Expect more news programming, spikes in news ratings, and **rising digital CPMs on news placements** as parties spend on Meta, Google and YouTube.
  - Election day is a day off.
  - Prefer lifestyle and non-news placements between 13 and 28 Oct, or schedule the launch around the window.
  - https://www.israelhayom.co.il/news/politics/article/21010609
- **TV price increases for 2026:** reported at about +10% on Keshet 12 and +7% on Reshet 13 prime-time news blocks. **(UNVERIFIED)** attribution.
  - https://www.israelhayom.co.il/tech/article/19700685
- **Security situation:**
  - 2026 saw repeated escalations.
  - The October 2023 precedent: commercial breaks disappeared for about a week.
  - Always negotiate pre-emption, make-good and pause clauses, keep "sensitive-period" creative ready, and keep part of the budget flexible (digital).
