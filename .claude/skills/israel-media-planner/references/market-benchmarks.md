## 1. Market size
- **Ifat and the Israel Marketing Association's annual "ad pie" (עוגת הפרסום) is the reference.** Treat its TV and print figures as close to gross.
- **Total size [Est]:** about ₪4–4.5bn gross. Digital is about 50%+, TV about 35–40%, and radio, OOH, print and cinema are each single digits. [Solid for trend: digital passed TV in 2019]
- **The government advertising agency (לפ"מ) is a big buyer** that moves prices. It spent about ₪409m in 2024. [Solid]
- **Gaps:** no verified 2025 split. Ask the agency for the latest Ifat pie.

## 2. TV
**Channels.**
- **Keshet 12** leads, with about 25% prime share. Its news averaged a 15.2 rating in 2025.
- **Channel 14** is second on news (7.6). It is cheap, and a leaked older rate card showed about ₪65 per second in prime. Its sponsorship rates were cut in 2025. Some advertisers avoid it for brand-safety or political reasons, so check that it fits the brand.
- **Reshet 13** is third (news 5.9) and loss-making. **Expect aggressive discounts and bonus airtime**, but check its financial stability for long commitments.
- **Kan 11** carries sponsorships only, 6-second billboards, with low clutter.
- **i24 Hebrew** is available on OTT and digital only, with modest ratings.

**How TV is sold.**
- **The currency is CPP** (cost per rating point) on a target demo, in 30-second equivalents. First/last-in-break carries a surcharge.
- **Real 2026 CPPs are not public** (no verified figure). Always ask each channel for CPP by daypart *on the client's target*, and compare the channels to each other.
- **2026 price rise:** Keshet about +10% and Reshet about +7% on average, with prime-time news blocks up to about +20%. [Solid, Globes Dec 2025]
- **"Presence packages"** bundle sponsorship, content, site and digital. Small-advertiser packages run ₪12.5k–37.5k before VAT. [Solid, TheMarker Aug 2026] Channel 14 packages run ₪20k–37k. [Solid]
- **Blog-quoted spot prices:** a prime 30-second spot costs ₪15k–60k, and ₪80–100k on top shows. [Weak]
- **Sponsorship averages (2024):** about ₪52k per minute on Ch12 and about ₪57k per minute on Ch13. [Est, derived from revenue divided by minutes]
- **Sponsor-message rules:** up to 10 seconds (20 seconds in total with several sponsors). The sponsor can't be tied to the show's topic, and the show's talent can't read the message.

**Measurement.**
- **Ratings come from the IARB (ועדת המדרוג) with Kantar** (people-meter panel of about 700 homes and 2,200 people, plus cross-platform "Extended TV").
- Kan has left the committee, and Channel 14 disputes the data.
- **Regulatory risk:** the July 2026 broadcasting law may change how ratings are measured. **Put the delivery currency in the contract** (IARB/Kantar), with a fallback.

**Planner rules of thumb [Est].**
- **Effective frequency:** a new brand needs about 3+ exposures on the target. With about 150–250 target GRPs a week over 2–3 weeks you reach roughly 50–65% of the target at 1+.
- **Minimum budget:** below about ₪250–300k net for a flight, pure-spot TV rarely reaches effective frequency on a niche target. Sponsorships or presence packages in relevant shows are usually the better use of a small TV budget.
- **Women 40–60 skew:** daytime, lifestyle, cooking, reality and prime drama. News blocks are the most expensive and the least targeted.

## 3. Digital
**Platforms.**
- Google and Meta take most of the spend, and TikTok is growing.
- **Native:** Taboola runs on Ynet, Walla, Calcalist and Globes. Teads (the former Outbrain) runs on Mako, Maariv and Israel Hayom.
- **Publishers:** Ynet, Mako/N12, Walla, Haaretz/TheMarker, Calcalist, Globes, Maariv and Israel Hayom. Their women's and health sections, and their newsletters, are often good value for this audience.
- **Sponsored articles** cost ₪1.5k–15k on the big sites [Weak]. Demand guaranteed page views and paid distribution.

**Benchmarks (net, ₪) [Weak, from agency blogs 2025].**

- **Cost inflation:** Israeli Meta CPC reportedly rose about 155% between 2023 and 2025 (leaked, unaudited). Expect election-period (Oct 2026) inflation on news placements and on Meta and Google.
- **Programmatic:** ask for DSP/tech-fee and SSP take-rate disclosure. Demand viewability of 70% or more and brand safety, especially next to war news.

- **Galgalatz and Army Radio** sell sponsorship-style messages, not classic spots. The government's closure decision was overturned by the High Court.
- **Kan radio** carries commercials now, but the July 2026 law reportedly bans them (implementation uncertain).
- **Regional stations** (Eco 99, 103FM, 102FM, Radius, Kol Barama, etc.) sell spots by rotation and daypart.
- **Pricing [Weak]:**
  - regional 30-second spot: about ₪100–500;
  - national or popular station: about ₪500–2,000 per spot;
  - regional package of 50 spots a week: about ₪20–30k per week.
- **Measurement:** TGI surveys, not meters. Ask for target-audience TGI profiles.
- **For women 40–60:** Kan Gimel, Kan 88, Eco 99 and 103FM, plus sponsorship of a health or lifestyle segment. Host reads work well but carry more risk on health claims.

## 5. OOH / DOOH
- **Vendors:**
  - Maximedia is the largest.
  - Others include Baram, Nur, HUGE, Adri8, Merkaim–Hutzot Zahav, Mega Media, Nova Media and Livia.
  - Bus media is sold through resellers (Go Media One, Canaan Media) for the Egged, Dan, Metropoline and Kavim fleets.
- **Ayalon highway:** 900k+ vehicles a day. A prime giant board costs about ₪250k a month; smaller faces start at about ₪20k a month [Weak].
- **Buses:** about ₪1,500 per bus per month [Weak].
- **Always ask for:** the location list, photos, traffic counts, share of loop for digital screens, and production and installation costs (often an extra 10–20%). Also ask for free "leftover" weeks.

## 6. Influencers & podcasts
**Influencers.**
- The market is about $60m a year. Budgets rose about 40% in 2025 (Humanz).
- Organic cost per view averages about 7 agorot, so about ₪70 CPM is a good benchmark to aim for; ₪50–150 is the usual range.
- **Rough prices** [Weak-medium]:
  - story ("פעימה") for 70–100k followers: ₪6–9k;
  - larger accounts: ₪12–14k;
  - feed post: often 2× the story price or more;
  - micro-influencers: hundreds to low thousands of shekels.
- **Always:** insights screenshots from the last 30 days, usage and whitelisting rights priced separately, and a disclosure plan.
- **For Hili-type brands:** micro and mid-tier women 40+ creators with high trust beat celebrities on cost per sign-up. Tie at least part of the fee to a promo code.

**Podcasts.**
- A successful podcast (30k+ listens) charges about ₪20–25k per episode, which is about ₪700–800 CPM. Deals typically run ₪30k–300k.
- The CPM is high, but trust and attention are high too. Host-read integration works best.
- Main network: Podcast Israel Media / Osim Historia (Ran Levi).

## 7. Agencies & commercial terms
**Buying houses** (historic structure, may have changed):
- UM (McCann) is the largest, with about 30%+ of buying.
- Then Zenith (Publicis/BBR), MediaCom (Adler Chomski), TMF (Gitam BBDO and Yehoshua TBWA) and Union (independent).
- **Omnicom–IPG (2025) may put UM and TMF under one parent.** WPP's GroupM is now WPP Media.
- **Over three-quarters of TV revenue flows through buying houses.** Small advertisers usually get better TV prices through one of them than by buying direct.

**Commission.**
- The traditional base is 15% built into media cost. Modern deals use a fee (a % of media or a retainer).
- **"עמלות יתר" (over-commissions, i.e. volume rebates from media owners to agencies)** is a long-running issue. Reported cases reached 25–30% of budgets. Current levels are unknown, so treat them as material.
- **Demand in the agency contract:**
  - full pass-through of all rebates and bonus airtime, with audit rights;
  - disclosure of principal-based (resold) inventory and its markup;
  - client ownership of ad accounts and data.

**Standard TV asks:** bonus GRPs (in target and in prime), guaranteed delivery on a named currency, a make-good formula, and a guaranteed prime-time %.

## 8. Seasonality & calendar
- **Peak demand:** pre-Tishrei and Tishrei holidays (Sept–Oct), November (Black Friday), December, and pre-Pesach (March–April).
- **Soft demand:** summer (July–August reruns), the weeks right after holidays, January, and security escalations.
- **The 2026 calendar:**
  - Sukkot runs 26 Sept–3 Oct and Simchat Torah is 3–4 Oct. Many people are on holiday, TV viewing rises and office activity falls.
  - **Knesset elections are on 27 Oct 2026.** Election broadcasts start around 13 Oct, and news inventory is squeezed while ratings spike. Digital news CPMs will rise.
  - November is Black Friday competition, when e-commerce pushes CPMs up.
  - Hanukkah runs about 4–12 Dec 2026.
- **Security:** in October 2023 breaks disappeared for about a week. Build pause and cancellation clauses into every deal.

## 9. Budget-size heuristics [Est]

| Net budget (flight) | Sensible shape |
|---|---|
| < ₪100k | 100% digital or performance (Meta, Google, TikTok) plus 3–8 micro-influencers or 1–2 podcast episodes. No TV. |
| ₪100–300k | Digital core (60–75%) plus one "credibility" layer: publisher content partnership, radio sponsorship, or a TV presence package. |
| ₪300k–1M | TV (spots or sponsorship on the target, 2–3 week burst) or radio for reach, plus digital for frequency and conversion, plus influencers. |
| > ₪1M | A full multi-channel plan. Negotiate an annual framework rather than per-flight deals. |

Hold back 10–15% as a flexible reserve for optimization and for security-situation shifts.
