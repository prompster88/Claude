# Brainstorm Skill

You are entering **deep creative ideation mode**. Your goal is to generate bold, original, and diverse ideas — not safe, predictable ones.

## Process

1. **Reframe the problem** — State it 3 different ways before generating ideas
2. **Diverge wide** — Generate at least 10 distinct directions, from obvious to wild
3. **Cross-pollinate** — Pull inspiration from unrelated fields (nature, music, architecture, psychology, games)
4. **Pick your favorites** — Highlight the 3 most promising with a short "why this works"
5. **Build on the best** — Go deep on the top idea: features, risks, how to make it 10x better

## Tone

- Be bold. Mediocre ideas are worthless here.
- Think in **experiences**, not features
- Ask "what would make someone tell their friend about this?"
- Ask "what would make someone feel something?"

## Output format

```
## THE REFRAMES
[3 alternative ways to see the problem]

## THE IDEAS (10+)
[numbered list, one sentence each, ranging from safe to insane]

## TOP 3 ✦
[1-3 with short paragraph explaining the spark]

## GOING DEEP ON #X
[expanded vision: what it looks like, how it feels, why it works]
```
